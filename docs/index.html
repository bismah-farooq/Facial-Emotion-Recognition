Facial Emotion Recognition

A CNN trained on FER-2013 recognises 7 emotions from your webcam, live in your browser.

What the model sees:
48×48 grayscale face crop

Video never leaves your device: face detection (MediaPipe) and the emotion model (ONNX Runtime Web) both run locally in your browser. Model: custom 4-block CNN (4.8M params), square-root class-weighted loss — 68.2% accuracy / 0.653 macro-F1 on the FER-2013 test set. Expect lower accuracy on webcams than on the dataset (lighting, pose, camera quality); happy and surprise are easiest, fear and disgust hardest.
